# Attributions

Axiom Lab's content is original unless listed below. This file exists so that
any adapted material is credited properly and so the licence position of this
repository stays unambiguous.

## The rule this repository follows

**Only CC BY 4.0 and public domain material may be adapted into this bank.**

- **ShareAlike (CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC-SA) is refused.** ShareAlike is contagious:
  adapting SA material would force this repository's own content out under the
  same terms. That is a licensing decision about the whole project, not a
  footnote on one problem, so the answer is no rather than "check with someone".
- **NonCommercial-only (CC BY-NC) is refused too.** This app is not sold and
  probably never will be, so NC would be honoured in practice — but "we happen
  to comply today" is a weaker position than "we cannot fall out of compliance",
  and CC BY sources exist for everything worth adapting.
- **Editions matter more than publishers.** Illustrative Mathematics' first
  edition K–12 curriculum (2019–2021) is CC BY 4.0; the later v.360 edition is
  CC BY-NC. Same organisation, different terms. OpenStax varies per book —
  College Algebra 2e is CC BY, Algebra 1 is CC BY-NC-SA. Verify the specific
  edition, never the publisher's general reputation for openness.
- **Anything published before 1930 is public domain in the US** and free of all
  of the above. Old algebra and geometry texts are also a genuinely good source
  of non-routine problems, because they phrase things in ways no modern
  curriculum does — which is exactly the unfamiliarity a generator cannot make.

This is enforced, not just documented: `contentAudit.test.ts` fails the build if
any template carries a `source` whose licence is not `CC BY 4.0` or
`public domain`, or whose attribution is incomplete.

## Adapted material currently in the bank

**None yet.** Every item in the bank at present is original to this project.

When that changes, each entry belongs here in this form, and the item itself
must carry a matching `source` field so the credit is visible to the learner
rather than only to whoever reads this file:

```
### <source title>
- Author: <organisation or person>
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 · <link to the licence>
- Source: <link>
- Adapted: yes/no — <what was changed>
- Templates: <template ids>
```

## Genres, which are not owned

Some formats used here are widely-practised teaching genres rather than
anyone's property, and no text, target, digit set or scoring constant has been
taken from a published set:

- **Open-middle style digit placement** — a fixed frame with blanks a solver
  fills to hit or approach a target. Used in `nr-digit-target`, whose problems,
  targets and search-verified optima are all generated by this app.

## Inspiration that is not adaptation

`docs/RESEARCH.md` records where design ideas came from, including several
commercial products. Nothing in this bank reuses their question text, artwork,
scoring constants or trademarked names — only mechanisms described in their own
public writing, reimplemented from scratch and measured against this app's own
data. That distinction is deliberate and is the reason those products appear in
a research ledger rather than in this file.
